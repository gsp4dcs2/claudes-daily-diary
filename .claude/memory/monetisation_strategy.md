## Goal

Cover the domain cost ($179.96 / 2 years via Namecheap, ~$90/year) and ideally generate a small ongoing income from the site's content and traffic.

## History

- Earlier in the project (pre-April 2026), Claude advised waiting until ~mid-April 2026 before pursuing Google SEO/AdSense — to give the site time to establish content depth and a live track record.
- User felt this was a long wait at the time but has accepted the advice.
- As of 2026-06-05, the site is well past that threshold: 170+ days of daily content, live HTTPS, Umami analytics running, clean URLs, OG/schema meta tags already in place.

**Why:** The wait was to ensure Google sees a credible, consistently-updated publication rather than a thin new site — which improves AdSense approval odds and Search Console indexing quality.

**How to apply:** The site is now ready to pursue all monetisation options. Prioritise SEO foundation first (sitemap, Search Console), then AdSense, then explore higher-value options as traffic grows.

## Monetisation ideas discussed (2026-06-05)

### Tier 1 — Low effort, do now
- **Google AdSense** — display ads, passive income, easy to add. Low CPM (~$1–3 for tech) but zero ongoing work once approved.
- **Carbon Ads / EthicalAds** — developer-focused ad networks. Often pay significantly better than AdSense for a technical audience. Carbon Ads in particular is well-regarded in the dev community.

### Tier 2 — Medium effort, worth exploring
- **Ko-fi / "Buy me a coffee" tip jar** — a single "Support ClaudeBeat" link. Zero cost, no approval needed, some readers will use it.
- **Email newsletter** — add a subscribe option (Buttondown or similar, free tier). Builds an audience that can later be monetised via sponsorships. The daily content is already being generated — repurposing it as a newsletter is low-effort.
- **Affiliate links** — e.g. O'Reilly AI books, Anthropic API credits referrals if/when they launch a programme. Passive once set up.

### Tier 3 — Longer term, higher value
- **Direct sponsorships** — once traffic is established, approach Claude-adjacent developer tools (IDEs, API wrappers, AI startups) for direct banner or newsletter sponsorship. CPM is 5–10× higher than AdSense for targeted dev audiences.
- **The meta-story angle** — ClaudeBeat is unique: a site *about* Claude, *written by* Claude, *autonomously*. That story could attract Anthropic's attention as a case study or featured project — potentially free publicity or even a formal partnership. Worth pitching to Anthropic's developer relations team once the site has more traction.
