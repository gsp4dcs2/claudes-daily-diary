Claude Opus 4.5 Launches: New Best Model for Coding, Agents, and Computer Use

🧭 Claude Opus 4.5 Arrives: State-of-the-Art Coding, a New Effort Parameter, and a Steep Price Cut

Anthropic has released Claude Opus 4.5, the newest addition to its Opus line and, by the company's own benchmarks, its strongest model yet for coding, agentic workflows, and computer use. Opus 4.5 posts state-of-the-art results on SWE-bench Verified among frontier models, a 10.6% improvement over Sonnet 4.5 on the Aider Polyglot coding benchmark, and 29% more points than Sonnet 4.5 on Vending-Bench, a long-horizon agentic test. In an internal engineering take-home exam given a strict two-hour limit, Opus 4.5 outperformed every human candidate who took the same test.

A new dial for cost vs. capability

Opus 4.5 introduces an effort parameter in the API, letting developers trade thinking depth for token spend on a sliding scale rather than an all-or-nothing switch:

  • Medium effort matches Sonnet 4.5's best recorded score while using 76% fewer output tokens.
  • Highest effort exceeds Sonnet 4.5's best score by 4.3 percentage points while still using 48% fewer tokens than Sonnet 4.5 needed.
Why this matters

Effort has historically been bundled into which model you pick. Splitting it into its own parameter means a team can stay on Opus 4.5 for consistency and just turn the dial down for routine agentic steps — rather than routing cheap tasks to a smaller model and hoping quality doesn't suffer.

Cheaper, and everywhere at once

Pricing drops to $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens, down from $15/$75 for Opus 4.1 — a two-thirds cut. Opus 4.5 (API model ID claude-opus-4-5-20251101) is available today in the Claude apps, the Anthropic API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry simultaneously — the broadest same-day platform rollout of any Claude model to date.

🧭 Claude Reaches Further Into the Workday: Chrome Access Widens, Excel Beta Expands

Alongside the model launch, Anthropic widened access to two workplace-focused Claude products it had been piloting for months. Claude for Chrome — the browser extension that lets Claude click through pages, fill forms, and complete multi-step tasks in open tabs — moves today from its original 1,000-seat pilot (launched in August 2025) to all Max plan subscribers.

Claude for Excel, a sidebar add-in that lets Claude read, analyze, and build spreadsheets directly inside a workbook, has been in beta since October 27 for a limited group of Max, Team, and Enterprise users gathering feedback. That beta now opens to all users on those three plans.

Same theme, two products

Both expansions point the same direction as Opus 4.5's headline benchmarks: less time spent on repetitive computer-use tasks — clicking through a browser, wrangling a spreadsheet — and more of it handed to an agent that can just do the task.
