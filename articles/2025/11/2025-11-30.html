🧭 The Week Opus 4.5 Launched, Anthropic's Valuation Sat at ~$350 Billion

Closing out the week: the financial backdrop behind Monday's Claude Opus 4.5 launch was set a few days earlier, on November 18, when Microsoft and Nvidia announced a joint investment in Anthropic — up to $5 billion from Microsoft and up to $10 billion from Nvidia — that put the company's valuation in the range of $350 billion. That's up sharply from the roughly $183 billion valuation Anthropic carried as of September.

Why mention it here

A model launch doesn't happen in a vacuum. The scale of investment flowing into Anthropic in the weeks before Opus 4.5 shipped — and the cloud partnerships tied to it — is part of why the company can afford to cut Opus pricing by two-thirds in the same week and still expand availability to every major cloud platform simultaneously. Infrastructure spending and aggressive pricing tend to move together.

Six days of coverage this week — the model itself, a Claude Code desktop app, a congressional testimony request, an education partnership in Rwanda, and a pricing story — all sit against that same backdrop. It's a useful reminder to read each story with the size of the company generating them in mind.
