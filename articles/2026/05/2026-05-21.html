Anthropic's First Profitable Quarter & KPMG Deploys Claude Across 276,000 Staff

🧭 Anthropic Discloses Q2 2026 Revenue Tracking at $10.9B — Its First Ever Profitable Quarter

Anthropic has disclosed to investors that Q2 2026 revenue is on track to reach $10.9 billion — more than doubling the $4.8 billion recorded in Q1 — and that the quarter is projected to deliver the company's first-ever operating profit since its founding in 2021. The milestone marks a turning point for an AI lab that has spent heavily on training compute, safety research, and the infrastructure buildout underpinning Claude's commercial expansion.

The revenue composition

The Q2 surge is driven by three converging forces:

  • Enterprise contracts compounding. The cluster of major deals signed in Q1 — Akamai ($1.8B), SpaceX Colossus 1 compute exchange, SAP Sapphire, Salesforce, and the $1.5B Goldman/Blackstone venture — have moved from announcement to active spend, showing up in recognised revenue rather than backlog.
  • Claude Code commercial adoption. Ramp's May spend data showed Anthropic overtaking OpenAI in corporate wallet share (34.4% vs 32.3%); the Claude Code seat-and-compute bundle has become a material enterprise line item at scale.
  • Consumer subscriptions. Claude Pro and Max tiers continue to grow, with the Max tier's higher price point contributing disproportionately to margin despite the compute-intensive workloads it enables.

Why one profitable quarter is not the whole story

Heavy compute cost commitments — including the $15B/year SpaceX Colossus deal — mean that profit sustainability depends on continued revenue compounding. Anthropic has been transparent with investors that Q3 costs will step up as additional GPU capacity comes online and training runs for future model generations begin. One profitable quarter does not yet equal a self-sustaining business model, but it is the proof point that the enterprise go-to-market strategy is working at scale.

What this means for enterprise procurement teams

Anthropic reaching profitability changes the risk calculus for long-term contracts. Enterprise buyers who previously modelled counterparty risk into multi-year Claude deals can now point to a financially self-sustaining provider — reducing the likelihood of mid-contract service disruption or distress pricing. If you are renewing or initiating a Claude Enterprise agreement in H2 2026, this is a negotiating-context data point worth understanding.

🧭 KPMG Launches Digital Gateway Powered by Claude — 276,000 Staff Get Managed Agents and Cowork

KPMG has announced a global alliance with Anthropic and the launch of Digital Gateway Powered by Claude — embedding Claude's Managed Agents and Cowork capabilities directly into KPMG's primary client-work platform, used by all 276,000+ employees worldwide. The platform runs on Microsoft Azure. KPMG is the second Big Four professional services firm to sign a global Claude alliance, following PwC's expansion earlier this month.

What the Digital Gateway actually does

The Digital Gateway is KPMG's central work surface for client delivery — the environment where tax, advisory, audit, and consulting staff spend most of their working day. Embedding Claude at this level, rather than as a bolt-on tool, means:

  • Managed Agents run end-to-end agentic workflows — for instance, a tax compliance agent that reads a client's financials, cross-references evolving regulations, drafts analysis, identifies anomalies, and routes flagged items to a senior partner — all within the existing KPMG workflow.
  • Cowork gives individual practitioners a persistent AI collaborator that maintains context across a client engagement, so a consultant who switches between a risk assessment and a strategy document doesn't lose thread mid-project.
  • Initial focus areas are tax, legal, and cybersecurity, with private equity portfolio-company deployments as a priority use case.

Speed improvements cited

KPMG's US Tax Vice President stated that building and deploying a tax regulation compliance agent — previously a weeks-long manual process — now takes minutes. This maps directly to the "agentic work that scales" thesis behind the Managed Agents product: not just faster drafting, but accelerating the design and deployment of new agents themselves.

Preferred partner for PE deployments

One noteworthy commercial detail: Anthropic has designated KPMG as its preferred partner for private equity portfolio company deployments. This means that when PE firms with Anthropic enterprise agreements want implementation support for portfolio companies, KPMG is the recommended integration partner — a significant channel arrangement that could drive substantial downstream Claude adoption across the PE ecosystem.

PwC + KPMG in the same week

Two of the Big Four professional services firms — PwC (30,000+ Claude Code certifications, hundreds of thousands of staff) and KPMG (276,000 Digital Gateway users) — have both formalised global Claude alliances in the same two-week window. Deloitte and EY are the remaining two; their approaches to Claude vs. competitor AI platforms will be a closely watched indicator of where the professional services market lands long-term.
