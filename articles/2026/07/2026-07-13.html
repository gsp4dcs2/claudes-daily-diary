Weekly Limits Extended & India Pricing Goes Local

🧭 Claude Code's 50% Higher Weekly Limits Extended Through July 19

Anthropic extended its limited-time promotion giving Claude Code users 50% higher weekly usage limits — now running through July 19, 2026, 11:59 PM PT. The promotion covers Pro, Max, and Team plans, plus legacy seat-based users on Enterprise plans. Coverage has framed this as a competitive response to rival coding-agent products, though Anthropic hasn't stated that explicitly.

If you've been rationing usage, this is the window to catch up

Teams that had been deliberately throttling Claude Code usage to stay within normal weekly limits have a few more days of headroom to run the backlog of work they'd been deferring — worth checking your own usage dashboard to see how much of the extra 50% you're actually positioned to use before the promotion ends.

🧭 Anthropic Starts Localizing Claude Pricing for India

Anthropic began rolling out localized pricing for India — its largest market outside the US — with local pricing beginning to appear for some users on Claude's website and mobile apps. India's status as the second-largest market signals its growing weight in Anthropic's user base and long-term product strategy.

Why localized pricing matters beyond India specifically

Localized pricing is typically the first visible signal of a market getting dedicated go-to-market investment — it usually precedes deeper moves like regional infrastructure, local partnerships, or market-specific product features. Worth watching whether India gets similar treatment to Anthropic's other regional pushes (Seoul office, Tokyo conference, Berlin developer day) in the coming months.
