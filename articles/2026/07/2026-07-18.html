Fable 5's Subscription Return Confirmed & Limits Extended to August 19

🧭 Double Announcement: Fable 5 Confirmed for Subscriptions July 20, Limits Boosted Through August 19

Anthropic confirmed Claude Fable 5 returns to Max and Team Premium plans on July 20, resolving weeks of staged, repeatedly-extended access since its June export-control suspension. In the same announcement, Claude Code's 50% higher weekly usage limits — previously set to lapse July 19 — were extended again, this time through August 19, 2026.

Reading the pattern across these repeated extensions

Anthropic's own framing (in earlier extensions) attributed the staged Fable 5 rollout to unpredictable demand and capacity constraints — a real, if unglamorous, explanation. The usage-limit extension pattern is different: it's now been extended multiple times without lapsing, which increasingly reads as a sustained competitive posture rather than a short-term promotion. Worth treating any future "limits end on X date" announcement with mild skepticism until it actually happens.
