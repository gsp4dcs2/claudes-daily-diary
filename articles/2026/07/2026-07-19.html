Don't Build Workflows Around Temporary Promotions

✅ A Simple Rule for Time-Limited Usage Boosts: Plan for the Floor, Not the Ceiling

This month's repeated extensions of Claude Code's weekly-limit boost are a useful case study: a promotion that's been extended three times so far is easy to start treating as permanent, but planning your team's normal-case workflow around it is a mistake the moment it actually lapses. A more resilient approach: design your default workflow assumptions around the standard (non-promotional) limits, and treat any boosted period as bonus headroom for catching up on deferred work — not as your new baseline.

Practical check before relying on any promotional terms

Before building automation or team processes that assume a promotional rate, boosted limit, or temporary access tier will persist, check the official terms directly rather than working from a headline — "extended again" coverage can lag the actual end date, and betting infrastructure on informal reporting is a bad trade for the small convenience it buys.
