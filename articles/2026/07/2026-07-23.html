Voice Mode Gets Model Choice & AMD's Reported $5B Bet

🧭 Claude Voice Mode Drops Its Fixed Haiku Backend for Model Choice

Claude's voice mode previously ran exclusively on Haiku — fast, but not well suited to complex requests spoken aloud. Anthropic updated voice mode so users can now choose between Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku models, with voice mode defaulting to the fastest version of whichever model you last used in text chat. The update follows a competitor's recent conversational-model and voice-mode refresh, a pattern of near-simultaneous voice feature updates across major AI labs this year.

Reconsider voice mode if you'd previously dismissed it

If you tried Claude's voice mode for a genuinely complex task and found the Haiku-only backend too shallow, it's worth another look now that Sonnet and Opus are available — the previous limitation was a backend model constraint, not a fundamental ceiling on what voice interaction can handle.

🧭 AMD Reportedly Planning Up to $5B Investment in Anthropic Alongside Chip Deal

Reports indicate AMD is planning to invest as much as $5 billion in Anthropic, paired with a chip supply agreement — joining a growing list of hardware and cloud vendors (Micron, Google, Broadcom among them) that have combined commercial deals with direct equity investment in Anthropic this year. As with any reported-but-not-yet-officially-confirmed deal, treat the specific figure as provisional until Anthropic or AMD confirm it directly.

The pattern is now well-established, not novel

Combining a compute/hardware supply relationship with an equity stake has become Anthropic's standard playbook for hardware partnerships (see Micron's June 22 deal). If confirmed, an AMD investment would extend that pattern to a second major chip vendor, diversifying Anthropic's hardware supply chain relationships beyond a single dominant partner — worth watching for the official confirmation and deal terms.
