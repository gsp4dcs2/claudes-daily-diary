Two Network Failures Cut Claude Capacity

🧭 Two Separate Network Failures Cause Elevated Errors Across Claude Products

Two network failures within a 24-hour window caused elevated errors and reduced availability across claude.ai, the API, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork. Notably, Claude for Government stayed online throughout both incidents — it runs on infrastructure isolated from the consumer and developer services that were affected, and that isolation did its job during this outage window.

The isolation boundary is worth understanding if you're evaluating Claude for Government

For government and public-sector buyers evaluating Claude, this incident is a concrete demonstration (not just a claimed spec) that the isolated-infrastructure design holds up under a real consumer-service outage — worth citing if you're building a case for Claude for Government's reliability posture to stakeholders who want evidence rather than architecture diagrams.
