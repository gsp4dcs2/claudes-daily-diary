🧭 Meet the Engineer Behind Claude Code — CNN Profiles Boris Cherny, Head of Applications at Anthropic

On November 15, CNN published an in-depth profile of Boris Cherny, the 35-year-old Anthropic engineer who created Claude Code. As the company heads into its IPO week, the story provides a rare window into the product thinking, craft mentality, and accidental origins behind what has become Anthropic's most commercially defining tool. Cherny — an engineer's engineer who relaxes by fermenting his own miso — joined Anthropic in September 2024 from Meta, where he spent years shipping developer-facing surfaces and testing new features with cafeteria workers to gauge intuitive usability.

What the CNN profile reveals

  • Title and scope: Cherny's official title is Head of Applications. He oversees not just Claude Code but the full surface layer where developers interact with Claude at the agent level — the CLI, IDE extensions, and the emerging agentic task substrate.
  • Design philosophy: He has described coding as "the reason most people choose to become engineers in the first place" — a belief that shapes Claude Code's minimalist UX. The goal was always to get out of the way and let Claude work, not to build another GUI on top of the model.
  • Cowork built in 10 days: The CNN piece confirms the widely-circulated anecdote that Anthropic built the entire Claude Cowork Suite — Meetings, Whiteboards, Sheets, and Data — in 10 days using Claude Code itself. Cherny was the internal champion who pushed for the team to dogfood the tool at that intensity.
  • Prior to Meta: Before Meta, Cherny worked on developer tooling in several early-stage contexts. He is also the creator of TypeScript Flow, an influential open-source type-checking tool that predates TypeScript's mainstream adoption.
Why this matters for Claude Code users

Cherny's cafeteria-worker usability test philosophy is directly visible in Claude Code's design: the tool ships very few explicit UI affordances and trusts Claude's planning and execution to do the heavy lifting. When something feels opaque in Claude Code, it is often intentionally so — Cherny's bet is that transparency through output (reading the diff, watching the tool call log) beats transparency through upfront configuration screens. This shapes how the product will evolve: expect less wizard-style onboarding and more in-context explanation of what Claude is doing and why.

🧭 Claude for Teachers Extended Through July 2028 — Free Premium Access Passes Two Million Educators

Anthropic updated the Claude for Teachers programme page on November 13 and confirmed the news more broadly on the 15th: the free premium access tier for verified K-12 educators in the United States will now run through July 30, 2028, extended from the original expiry of July 2027. The announcement coincides with a milestone: the programme has now crossed two million active educator accounts since its July 2026 launch, making it the fastest-growing educator AI initiative in the US K-12 segment.

What the extension covers

  • Free tier: Verified US K-12 teachers retain access to Claude Pro capabilities — extended context, priority throughput, and Projects — at no cost through July 2028. Verification still happens through the existing educator ID check at claude.ai/teachers.
  • No action required: Existing enrolled educators are automatically rolled forward. No new sign-up or re-verification is needed until the extended expiry.
  • New curriculum tools: The update also ships three new templates to the Claude for Teachers library: a standards-mapping assistant (links lesson plans to Common Core and state standards), a differentiated-instruction scaffolder (generates tiered versions of a lesson for different reading levels), and a parent-communication drafter.
  • Binding privacy protections in force: As of November 12, Anthropic's agreement with the American Federation of Teachers and the United Federation of Teachers makes the AI Safety and Privacy Standard for Schools contractually enforceable across all school districts using Claude for Teachers. No student data is used for model training; a FERPA-aligned K-12 Data Processing Addendum applies to all district deployments.
How to enrol or confirm your enrolment

Visit claude.ai/teachers and sign in with a school-issued email address. The verification flow takes roughly 90 seconds. Once verified, your account is automatically upgraded to Pro capabilities and the extended 2028 expiry. Administrators deploying district-wide should contact Anthropic's education team at education@anthropic.com to set up a district-level agreement that includes the binding privacy addendum.

🧭 Anthropic S-1/A Confirms IPO Price Range at $70–$78 Per Share — Valuation Up to $1.41 Trillion, Trading Starts November 19

With final pricing due November 18 and Nasdaq trading under ticker ANT set for November 19, Anthropic's amended S-1/A filing now formally sets the IPO price range at $70–$78 per share. At the top of the range the offering would raise approximately $64.4 billion, implying a fully diluted valuation between $1.26 trillion and $1.41 trillion — significantly above the preliminary range floated during the roadshow preparation. The offering covers 825 million shares, of which 801.4 million are newly issued by Anthropic and 23.6 million are sold by existing stockholders.

Key figures from the S-1/A

  • Price range: $70–$78 per share (Class A common stock)
  • Shares on offer: 825,000,000 total (801,351,354 primary + 23,648,646 secondary)
  • Gross proceeds at top of range: ~$64.4 billion, the largest US tech IPO since Saudi Aramco
  • Private placement tranche: A simultaneous $26 billion private placement at the IPO price to seven strategic partners including Alphabet, Nvidia, Microsoft, and TSMC — bringing total combined capital raised to ~$90.4 billion
  • Underwriters: Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, and JP Morgan lead the book
  • Voting structure: Class A shares carry one vote; Class B (held by founders and early employees) carry ten votes; Class C shares (held by strategic partners in the private tranche) carry no votes
What the price range means in context

The $70–$78 band represents a substantial upward revision from the initial indicative range circulated before roadshow launch, reflecting strong institutional demand from the moment the roadshow went live. The book closed multiple times oversubscribed within days of opening. For Anthropic, the final pricing on November 18 will determine whether the company achieves a valuation inside or above the $1.4T ceiling — analysts covering the roadshow expect the final print to land at or above the top of the stated range given reported demand.
